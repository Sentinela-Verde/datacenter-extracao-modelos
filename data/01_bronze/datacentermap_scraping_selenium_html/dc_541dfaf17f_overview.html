Rua Sebastião Cardoso:

Paulínia Data Center has 86,000 square feet of raised floor space and 16 MW of total power capacity, with an owned electrical substation and direct connection with the main PIX in Brazil.

Paulínia has become a strategic city after attracting one of the Latin America's largest petrochemical center. Paulínia Data Center is based in this privileged location. The site is located on the city's main highway, which provides quick access to the state capital and other cities across the region.
